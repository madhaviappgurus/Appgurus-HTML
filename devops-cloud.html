Risk Avoidance

Six Common Failure Patterns You Can Prevent

In the following most common failures, there is least problem of any technicality but it is an outcome of poor or no planning with a technical symptom. Here is a brief insight on what kind of problems we come across while engaging with clients and how we tackle it right from the beginning.

01

There was hardly any audit before commencing the build.

A very impulse-led and hasty decisions were taken for building tools without any proper mapping of the existing system which resulted in doubling the problem instead of solving it.

How We Can Prevent It:

At App Gurus, every engagement begins with a quick discovery which maps the entire existing system and identifies loopholes in the same.

02

Increase in Cloud Costs.

Without any proper control and monitoring from the beginning, cloud bill goes on increasing very quietly until the invoice lands.

How We Can Prevent It:

We recommend to introduce FinOps Control, tagging strategy and budget alerts to be active before initiating the first workload.

03

Security and Compliance Control

What if, after the 'Go-Live' stage, the SAST, DAST and Compliance controls are comparatively slower, costlier and incomplete.

How We Can Prevent It:

Security gates run in CI/CD from sprint one — OWASP, Snyk and SonarQube built in, not bolted on.

04

Tribal knowledge walks out the door

Infrastructure without a reliable documentation or IaC always results in permanent dependance on the team or engineers who have built it.

How We Can Prevent It:

At App Gurus, everything is documented in Terraform with complete runbook and architecture docs and they are handed over to the client before we sign off.

05

Big-bang migrations break things

Creating Multiple segments of different workloads at a time results in cascading failures with no clear rollback.

How We Can Prevent It:

Ideally the migration should be phased with a parallel run intervals and tested rollback for every wave and at every time and juncture.

06

When the vendors are not available post-delivery.

What if, at the end of the engagement and at the time of delivery there is no monitoring, no runbooks and no assistance provided by the vendor.

How We Can Prevent It:

An optional retainer can be agreed upfront with the inclusion of monitoring, patching and incident response post-delivery.

How We Build It

You are at Freedom to own what we document.

  • Everything we document be it reports, terraform state or accounts belong to you.
